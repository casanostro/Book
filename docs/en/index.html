ABOUT

Before the systems, a way of working

I started on a warehouse dock, not behind a dashboard. That is probably what best defines how I do data work: I never model a flow I haven't watched run. Every role since has added a layer — operations, ERP, BI, automation, AI — but the logic hasn't changed: understand the floor first, build the tooling second.

principle_01

The floor before the slides

A number that contradicts the teams' experience deserves an investigation, not a presentation. The best models I've built came out of conversations with the people who live the flow every day.

principle_02

Sobriety is a feature

I build systems teams can own without me: fewer dependencies, fewer layers, less magic. If a 50-line script is enough, there will be no platform.

principle_03

Say what the numbers say

Quantifying a risk also means owning it in front of leadership — including when the answer is uncomfortable. A wrong but reassuring indicator costs more than a right but inconvenient one.

principle_04

Documenting is handing over

A system only I can understand is a failure, not an achievement. Documentation is not the end of the project: it's what lets the project outlive me.

[05]

Root-cause investigation of a critical calculation bug

SQLRoot causeData quality

End-to-end diagnosis of a silent anomaly in an order simulation engine that distorted a service-level KPI published in reporting — without triggering a single alert.

Confirmed root cause: a fallback table frozen since July 2022 (~3.6M frozen historical rows). 83–85% of simulated lines were silently falling back to this stale source.

Impact — 0.53% of the expected volume actually generated (54 parcels vs ~10,244). Impact quantified, fix proposed and documented.
