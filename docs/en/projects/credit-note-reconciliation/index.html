CASE 09 Recovery delivered Silent blockage

The flow was running, the credit notes never arrived

Stores requested, advisers approved, the automation ran. Nobody saw an error. And yet part of the credit notes never reached invoicing.

Only a third matched on their own. For the other 75, the question was not where the error was, but whether any trace of the credit note existed at all.

Investigation

Four systems, one truth to rebuild

01 · Signal

Stores chasing their credit notes

Franchisees chase approved credit notes they never see. The automation flow reports no error. Starting point: take all 140 requests of the period, without assuming where they get lost.

02 · Cascade

Follow each request step by step

Form → tracking base: 127 of 140, the 13 gaps explained (refusals, manual duplicates). Approved: 112. Automatic match with invoicing on the label: 37. Left: 75 rows for which entry files were missing over the period.

Every step of the cascade has its own identifier and its own holes. The join key had to be rebuilt from the store code and the submission ID.

03 · Cause

One migration, one cut transmission

During a technical migration in January, the step that generates entry files for the team posting credit notes stopped working properly. No alert: requests stayed "approved", they simply stopped being sent.

04 · Recovery

Check by hand what will not match

The 75 remaining rows checked one by one, with a folder of supporting documents linked directly for the posting team. Result: 21 already invoiced (30 k€), 48 never posted (91 k€), 6 "similar" confirmed posted (7.4 k€). Duplicate check before any re-entry.

05 · Prevention

Make the blockage loud

Flow restored and stabilised. Proposed an intermediate "Pending" status: a request approved but not sent becomes visible instead of silently staying "approved". The BI credit-note report that followed exposes approved-but-unsent notes, which removes a monthly manual chase.
