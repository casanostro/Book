PROJECT [05]

Walking the lineage upstream — anatomy of an investigation

A service-level KPI published every week, wrong for months, with no alert ever firing. The investigation walked the calculation chain back to a fallback table frozen since July 2022 — onto which 83–85% of simulated lines were silently falling back.

ARCHITECTURE : FROM SYMPTOM TO ROOT CAUSE

THE METHOD

An investigation, not an intuition

method_01

Quantify before accusing

Every hypothesis is tested with a replayable query: expected volume (~10,244 parcels) against generated volume (54), then the exact share of lines hitting the fallback (83–85%). The diagnosis holds because every number can be re-run.

method_02

The silence was the real bug

The frozen table is a mistake; the fallback with no alert and no log is the design flaw. The proposed fix addresses both: reactivate the source, and make any future fallback loud — a silent fallback is debt paid back in trust.
