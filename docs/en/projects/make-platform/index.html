NODE : PROJECT [02]

Make platform — making silent failures visible

159 automation scenarios in production, 6 business domains — and an API migration that made thousands of executions fail without triggering a single alert. The job: turn an opaque dead letter queue into quantified exposure and a prioritised replay plan.

NODE : THE METHOD

From an opaque failure queue to a defensible action plan

step_01

Extract, don't sample

Systematic extraction of the execution history through the Make API, across the whole estate: all 5,376 failures are counted one by one, not estimated. A partial diagnosis would have produced a partial prioritisation.

step_02

Classify by business domain

Every failure is mapped to its domain — commercial gestures, quality alerts, missing-items base… — because failures are not equal: a lost commercial gesture has a direct cost, a missed alert a risk cost.

step_03

Quantify the exposure in euros

Failed executions carrying financial stakes are valued line by line: €27,625 of exposure traced. That figure — not the failure count — is what allows replay trade-offs to be made with the business.

step_04

Prioritise, replay, close the loop

Replays are ordered by decreasing impact and re-run in the fixed estate. The loop is closed: the same extraction mechanism then serves as monitoring, so the DLQ never becomes a blind spot again.
