how it works

The engine is triple exponential smoothing (additive Holt-Winters): it decomposes your history into a level, a trend and a seasonal profile, updated at every observed period.

Its three smoothing parameters are chosen automatically by grid search: the tool holds out the end of your history, tests hundreds of combinations and keeps the one that would have best predicted that unseen data (lowest MAPE) — then retrains on the full history to produce the final forecast. The displayed interval is ±1.96 standard deviations of the calibration errors.

Seasonality (weekly, monthly or daily) is detected from the spacing of your dates. At least two full cycles — two years for weekly or monthly data — are needed for a seasonal profile to be estimable.
