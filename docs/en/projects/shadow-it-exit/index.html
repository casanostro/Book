CASE 11 Specification written IT costing pending

The critical process was running on a POC

Daily stock-out tracking relied on a stack of no-code tools built as a proof of concept. It had become critical without anyone deciding so.

Three teams make weekly stock-out decisions on this base. Not a single component is under IT operations monitoring. The day it breaks, nobody else sees it.

Investigation

From a fragile POC to a specification

01 · Incident

Fragility, revealed by an update

An API change at the tracking-base vendor takes down every connected flow. Eight days of repair, while learning the tool at the same time. The symptom of a critical process with no safety net.

02 · Audit

Read the scenarios field by field

Audit of the missing-items scenarios: 13 date fields sent empty, erasing the existing value on every run, and update modules omitting 5 to 19 fields depending on the route. An archiving scenario failed on 99.97 % of its runs.

The defect was not in the database but in the mapping. Fixing it takes an hour; finding it meant reading every module.

03 · Risks

What the target must not inherit

A dedicated annex on the POC's limits: supplier attachments exposed through a public link, personal data in file names, evidence overwritten by each new email, no alert on an unmatched message, dependence on a named mailbox and an external converter under quota.

04 · Target

One shared tool, a locked structure

Web platform with SSO, one tab per product family, structure locked by administrators, free columns per team (Merchandising, Purchasing, Replenishment), owner and reason as drop-downs, supplier evidence one click away, autosave with author and timestamp.

05 · History

Turn comments into data

Every evening, the full state is written to the data warehouse. Reasons and comments become usable: in time, they pre-fill the comments of the weekly service-level newsletter instead of being rewritten.

This is the bridge to AI: a structured history of stock-out reasons is exactly what a model needs to propose a first comment that a human validates.
