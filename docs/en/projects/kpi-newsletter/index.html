NODE : WHAT IT CHANGES

Automate the mechanics, keep the judgment

choice_01

Everything repetitive is scripted

Extraction of the 9 sources, consistency checks, variance calculations, HTML formatting: the mechanical part of the newsletter no longer touches a human. That part cost 4 hours — and produced the copy-paste errors.

choice_02

The commentary stays editorial

The remaining 15 minutes are exactly where a human adds value: reading the numbers, writing the week's commentary, injecting it through a dedicated script. Automation didn't replace the analysis — it gave it its time back.
