    THE APPROACH

    Why two dashboards and not one

    audience_01, c-level

    A reading, not an exploration

    Five numbers, one trend, one breakdown, one sentence. No filters: if the reader has to operate the tool to understand, the design has failed. The month-over-month change carries the message; the detail is asked for in the meeting, not in the dashboard.

    audience_02, operations

    Actionable before beautiful

    The teams live in this screen: cross filters, sortable table, today's alerts on top. The density is a deliberate choice — what matters is that the line to handle first is visible first. Same data, inverted hierarchy.
